The colleges sent armies. Siege engines. Heroes. The Wall took them all, and grew taller on the bones.

BESTIARY

field notes of the last mages

Levy Footman

Pressed into the warlords' service for a copper a day. He raises his sword high — a white flash — then lunges in a straight line.

The lunge cannot turn. Step aside, jump it, or dash straight through.

Longbowman

Keeps his distance and drops arrows on you in long, lazy arcs. Backs away when you close in — but slower than you run.

The arc takes time. Move, and the arrow lands where you were.

Wall Ballista

A siege engine bolted to the stone, crewed by no one you can see. Its arm flashes white before the bolt flies — flat, fast, and true.

It cannot fire through stone. Duck below its line, or dash the instant it flashes.

The Catapult Master

Quartermaster of the siege camp, wed to his machine. He keeps his distance and drops burning sky on you. The engine is timber and tar — it burns beautifully.

Watch where the boulder's shadow falls, and stand where it isn't. Fire turns his own machine against him.

The Warden of Vhal

The Wall's last oath-keeper, armored in a hundred years of victories. He fights at spear's length: javelin volleys, a ground-splitting slam, and a charge like a falling gate.

Every assault leaves him winded — when the plume burns bright, he takes double. That is your window.
